Safety Settings

Stop sending follow-ups, if the reply is received

It will stop sending follow-up emails once the recipient replies, so they don't get unnecessary emails and you can continue your conversation directly.

Track email opens

It lets you see if your email was opened. However, tracking emails can sometimes cause your email to end up in the "Promotions" or "Spam".

Remove Styling Automatically

Send your emails without any special formatting, like colors or fonts. This makes them look more human-like and helps them land directly in the inbox. Styling is also removed from content you paste, including in spintax variants.

Only trigger one subsequence per prospect

A prospect will enter only one matching subsequence and will never move into another subsequence of this sequence.

Send emails as text only

Sends emails without any HTML, images or links, matching what a person would type by hand.

Only first emailRECOMMENDED

Sends your first email as plain text to improve inbox placement, while still being able to include demo links in follow-ups.

All emails

Sends all emails as text only, without any HTML, images, or links. This helps them land in the inbox more easily.

What changes on this screen

One sentence added to the Remove Styling description (highlighted). Everything else stays as it is.
